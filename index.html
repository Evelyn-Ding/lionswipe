Help & About

What LionSwipe Does

LionSwipe helps Columbia students get more out of their meal plan. It shows real Columbia dining hall menus, searches nearby off-campus restaurants and looks up their actual current prices, and tracks your dining swipes and off-campus spending so you always know where you stand for the semester.

What Info We Ask For

To track your plan, we ask how many swipes your meal plan has and when your semester starts and ends (or you can pick a preset plan). From there you just log dining hall swipes and off-campus purchases as you go, and you can optionally set a spending goal by day, week, month, or semester. You can use LionSwipe as a guest with no sign-up — your data stays in your browser until you create an account, at which point we offer to migrate it over.

The Problem We're Solving

It's easy to lose track of how many meal swipes you have left, overspend on off-campus food without noticing, and not know what's actually on the dining hall menu until you're standing in line. LionSwipe puts real menus, real off-campus prices, and a running tally of swipes and spending in one place, so you can make quick, informed food decisions.
